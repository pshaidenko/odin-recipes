Perfect Apple Pie Recipe

This recipe is my adaptation of apple pie recipe by Carol, originally published on Allrecipes.

It is inspired by the classic apple pie and combines a flaky, buttery crust with a sweet and flavorful apple filling.

I chose this recipe as part of my Odin Recipes project to practice my HTML skills while creating a recipe page.

Ingredients:

  • 1 (14.1 ounce) package pastry for a 9-inch double-crust pie

  • ¾ cup white sugar, or more to taste

  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon

  • 6 cups thinly sliced apples

Instructions

  1. Gather the ingredients. Preheat the oven to 450 degrees F (230 degrees C).

  2. Line a 9-inch pie dish with one pastry crust; set the second one aside.

  3. Prepare the apple filling by mixing sliced apples, sugar, and cinnamon in a large bowl.

  4. Fill the pie crust with the apple mixture and cover with the second pastry crust.

  5. Bake the pie on the lowest rack of the preheated oven for 10 minutes. Reduce the oven temperature to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C) and continue baking until the crust is golden brown and the filling is bubbling, 30 to 35 minutes more.

Serve and enjoy!
